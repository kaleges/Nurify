Delete Your Nurify Account & Data

This page explains how to request deletion of your Nurify account (developed by Kaleges) and the data associated with it.

Option 1 — Delete from within the app

  1. Open Nurify.
  2. Go to Settings → Account.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

This immediately removes your authentication record and any cloud-synced data.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, email us from the address linked to your account:

📧 info@kaleges.com
Subject: Delete my Nurify account

We will verify your request and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What data is deleted

  • Account information — email address, display name, and user ID (Firebase Authentication).
  • Cloud-synced content — prayer logs, dhikr counts, fasting records, bookmarks and preferences (Firebase Firestore).

What is kept (and for how long)

  • Data stored only on your device is removed when you uninstall the app.
  • We may retain minimal, anonymized diagnostic/analytics records and any information required for legal, security, or fraud-prevention purposes for up to 90 days, after which it is deleted.
